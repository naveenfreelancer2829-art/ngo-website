About Us

Organisation profile

Founded in 2012 by a group of teachers and doctors from Bengaluru, Seva Jyothi Foundation began as a weekend tuition centre in one village. Today it is a registered trust with 64 staff and over 300 volunteers, working with government schools, primary health centres and gram panchayats.

Vision

A rural India where every child learns, every family is healthy and every adult can earn a dignified living.

Mission

To deliver quality education, preventive healthcare and livelihood training to underserved communities through local partnerships and transparent use of funds.

Objectives

  • Improve learning outcomes in government schools
  • Provide free primary health screening
  • Train women and youth in employable skills
  • Promote clean water and sanitation
  • Support environmental conservation

Background

Registered under the Indian Trusts Act on 14 March 2012 in Bengaluru. Areas of work: Ramanagara, Tumakuru, Chikkaballapur and Kolar districts of Karnataka.
